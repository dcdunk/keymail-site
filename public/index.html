Features

Everything you do in email, one key away.

Built around the habits of inbox zero: triage fast, clear what's done, bring back what matters when it matters.

⌘K

Command palette

Type any action. Each result shows its shortcut, so you learn them as you go.

E

Mark Done

Archive in one keystroke. The next message is already open.

⌘Ksnooze

Snooze

Hide a thread until later. It comes back to the top of your inbox on time.

HL

Split Inbox

Tabs built from rules on sender, domain, recipient, subject or label.

⌘Ksend later

Send later & follow-ups

Schedule a send. Get the thread back if nobody replies in N days.

⌘Ksnippet

Snippets & unsubscribe

Saved replies with variables. One-key unsubscribe from lists.

Privacy

Your mail stays between you and Google.

01

No servers in the middle

Keymail talks to the Gmail API directly from your Mac. There's no Keymail backend reading or storing your email.

02

Sign in with Google

You sign in through Google in your browser. Keymail never sees your password. Tokens live in the macOS Keychain.

03

No tracking pixels

Keymail doesn't add read receipts to the mail you send. What you send is what they get.
