Privacy

Your mail stays between you and your provider.

Gmail

Any number of Google accounts, including Google Workspace.

iCloud Mail

Your iCloud address, plus aliases to send from.

01

No servers in the middle

Keymail connects straight from your Mac to Gmail and iCloud. There's no Keymail backend reading or storing your email.

02

Your passwords stay yours

Gmail signs in through Google in your browser. iCloud uses an app-specific password, never your Apple Account password. Both stay in your Mac's Keychain.

03

No tracking pixels

Keymail doesn't add read receipts to the mail you send. What you send is what they get.
