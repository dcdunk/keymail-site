Privacy

Your mail stays between you and your provider.

Gmail

Any number of Google accounts, including Google Workspace.

iCloud Mail

Your iCloud address, plus aliases to send from.

Outlook Preview

Outlook.com, Hotmail and Microsoft 365 work or school accounts.

Yahoo Mail Preview

Your Yahoo address, with an app password from Yahoo.

01

No servers in the middle

Keymail connects straight from your Mac to your mail provider. There's no Keymail backend reading or storing your email.

02

Your passwords stay yours

Gmail and Outlook sign in through Google and Microsoft in your browser. iCloud and Yahoo use an app password, never your account password. All of it stays in your Mac's Keychain.

03

No tracking pixels

Keymail doesn't add read receipts to the mail you send. What you send is what they get.
