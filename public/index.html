Privacy

Your mail stays between you and Google.

01

No servers in the middle

Keymail talks to the Gmail API directly from your Mac. There's no Keymail backend reading or storing your email.

02

Sign in with Google

You sign in through Google in your browser. Keymail never sees your password. Tokens live in the macOS Keychain.

03

No tracking pixels

Keymail doesn't add read receipts to the mail you send. What you send is what they get.
