Roadmap

What's coming next

What we're planning for Keymail. Order and timing may change as we hear from you. For what has already shipped, see the release notes.

Keymail Roadmap

Request a feature →
  • PlannedMac

    Desktop widgets

    Unread counts and your newest mail on the desktop or in Notification Center.

  • PlannedAccounts

    Any IMAP account

    Fastmail, custom domains and any other IMAP server, next to Gmail, iCloud, Outlook and Yahoo.

  • PlannedSpeed

    Instant delivery

    New mail pushed to your Mac the moment it lands, instead of checked every few seconds.

  • PlannedReliability

    Send Later and Snooze, even when Keymail is closed

    A small background helper sends scheduled mail and brings snoozed threads back on time.

  • PlannedInbox

    Mail rules

    Match on sender, subject, text or mailing list, then move, archive, mark read or trash as mail arrives.

  • PlannedNotifications

    Sign-in codes, one click

    Verification codes show in the notification with a Copy button, so you never open the email.

  • PlannedPrivacy

    Link tracking removed

    Tracking tags stripped from the links you open, so senders can't see who clicked.

  • PlannedAI

    On-device AI

    Summaries and rewrites with Apple Intelligence on your Mac, no AI app or subscription needed.

  • PlannedMac

    More languages

    Keymail in your Mac's language, starting with the most requested ones.
